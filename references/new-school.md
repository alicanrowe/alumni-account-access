# The New School (including Parsons)

Checked on 2026-11-20 from New School IT and registrar pages. Re-check anything older than 6 months before relying on it.

## What alumni keep and lose

Source: Graduating Students Transition Guide, https://services.newschool.edu/TDClient/32/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=1107

- 30 days after degree conferral, every university service ends except the school email. Files in Drive, Office 365, Adobe and Zoom are permanently deleted.
- The school email (netid@newschool.edu, on Google) stays as long as the graduate signs in at least once every 6 months with Google 2-Step Verification turned on.
- Ends 30 days after conferral: MyNewSchool, Google Drive, Calendar and other Google services, Duo MFA, Adobe, print points, Cloud Desktop, lab computers, Qualtrics, Gartner.
- The ID card is disabled on conferral. Equipment Center access ends the day after the last class.
- Google 2-step had to be set up before conferral. Without it, the graduate loses access and has to go through IT Central for identity verification, which "can take several days."

## Signing in as an alumnus

Source: Alumni email article, https://services.newschool.edu/TDClient/32/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=1023

1. Go to mail.google.com (not the MyNewSchool portal, which is closed to alumni).
2. Enter the full netid@newschool.edu address and the New School password.
3. Complete Google 2-step (not Duo).

Graduates from before spring 2014 can request an alumni account at https://form.jotform.com/200304893736153. New alumni accounts must set up 2-step within a week.

## Password reset and lost 2-step

The reset at account.newschool.edu sends a 6-digit PIN to the personal recovery email on file (shown masked, like e*****e@g***l.com). The PIN expires in 30 minutes. If the graduate can't reach that recovery address, the self-service reset can't finish, and only IT Central can change the recovery address after verifying identity. The registrar can't, and will send them to IT Central.


Source: Alumni email FAQ, https://services.newschool.edu/TDClient/32/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=1024

- Reset the password at https://account.newschool.edu. It asks for the NetID (the part of the email before the @, not the N-number), and verifies identity with date of birth and the last 4 digits of the SSN or ITIN. The graduate types those in themselves.
- Lost phone: sign the lost phone out of the Google account, change the password at account.newschool.edu, then choose "Try another way" and use a backup code, another phone or a security key.
- If nothing works, contact IT Central.

## Transcripts, verification and diplomas

- Official transcripts: ordered online through the National Student Clearinghouse at https://tsorder.studentclearinghouse.org/school/select, paid by credit card, in print or electronic form. The registrar's auto-reply (Oct 2026) says no student ID or SSN is needed to order, and a free unofficial PDF transcript can be ordered for yourself the same way. No school login is needed. Help: https://nscsso.my.site.com/student/s/
- Registrar help: reghelp@newschool.edu. Hours Monday to Thursday 10 a.m. to 5:45 p.m., Friday 10 a.m. to 4:45 p.m. ET. The auto-reply links a guide to finding a lost N-number and NetID.
- Registrar's Office: 212-229-5620.
- Degree verification, degree conferral and diploma services: creditanddegree@newschool.edu. https://www.newschool.edu/registrar/verifications
- Replacement diploma: registrar form, fee applies, marked "Reissued." https://www.newschool.edu/registrar/diplomas
- Certified electronic diploma (CeCredential): https://www.newschool.edu/registrar/certified-electronic-credential-diploma. It is sent to the school email, so it needs a working school login.
